About Avanya

North Bengal's digital discovery platform for tourism stays and real estate — built by people who know these hills, not a national platform that added a North Bengal filter.

Our story

Avanya started from a simple frustration: North Bengal — Darjeeling, Kalimpong, Kurseong, Mirik, Dooars, Lava, and Kaffer — has always had a wealth of homestays, tea bungalows, farmland, and heritage properties, but no single, trustworthy place to discover them without wading through unrelated national listings or word-of-mouth alone.

We built Avanya as a discovery-first platform: browse freely across two verticals, tourism stays and real estate, and reach out directly to the people behind each listing. No booking engine, no online payments — just a clear, honest way to find what actually exists across our seven locations, and a direct line to enquire.

Our mission

To be the most trustworthy digital front door to North Bengal's tourism and real estate landscape — organised by the region's real geography and property types, not generic categories borrowed from elsewhere in the country.
